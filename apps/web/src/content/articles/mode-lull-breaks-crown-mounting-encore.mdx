Mode Designs' row on thock's trends tracker spent the back half of 2026 doing two very different things: climbing to a near-ceiling score, then sitting still for six straight weeks near the floor it fell to. The 2026-W47 snapshot, published 2026-11-16, is the first week since that fall began that the number actually moved up — 68 to 71. What moved it is the interesting part. Nothing restocked. No board shipped. No new colorway dropped. PC Gamer reported that the next refresh of Mode's <Mono>Encore</Mono> will carry over the <Mono>Crown Mounting System</Mono>, a feature that debuted on an entirely different Mode board. Whether that counts as the lull ending or just its first visible crack is worth sitting with for another tracker week or two before calling it either way.

The run, in order: 98 in 2026-W40, 95 in W41, 72 in W42, 72 in W43, 70 in W44, 69 in W45, 68 in W46, 71 in W47. The shape is a staircase down, not a cliff — most of the damage happened in a single two-week window, W41 into W42, and the rest of the run is six weeks of the tracker essentially flatlining a few points above its floor.

The W40 ceiling had a clean cause: Mode's <Mono>Prologue</Mono> opened preorders on September 17, 2026, at 98 percent funded, pricing from $349, and introduced the Crown Mounting System as a new feature on that board specifically. A week later, in W41, Mode's own Summer 2026 update kept the score near its high by confirming shipping status across the lineup — Sonnet and Encore updates, Prologue progress, a final-stock call for <Mono>Envoy</Mono>, and Sonnet 2026 units already in customers' hands. That update was the last concrete news item for six weeks. W42's note mentions a 2026 Sonnet refresh teased inside Mode's own compendium, but the tracker read the post-fulfillment lull as the dominant story that week regardless, and the score fell to 72. From W43 through W46, the note repeats itself with only cosmetic changes: Sonnet and Encore remain in a post-group-buy fulfillment lull, Encore's summer shipment already out the door, no restock, no teaser, no new colorway. The score drifted down a point most weeks — 72, 70, 69, 68 — simply because nothing was happening to hold it up.

## The feature isn't new — it's just new to Encore

The report that moved the score is easy to mis-read as Encore news. It is more precisely Prologue news arriving a second time, now attached to a different board. <Mono>Crown Mounting System</Mono> debuted on the <Mono>Prologue</Mono> in W40 — it's the feature that helped that board hit 98 percent funded in the first place. It is not the same thing as the mounting hardware inside the <PartReference id="sonnet">Mode Sonnet</PartReference>, which uses what Mode calls the Mounting Block System: EPU41 polymer blocks that capture the plate's tabs in place of a silicone gasket strip, producing the soft, balanced feel the hobby still files under "gasket-mount" even though it isn't one structurally. That system is older, Sonnet-era, and nothing in the W47 report touches it.

<Callout type="note" title="Two mounting systems, not one upgraded">
  Mode now has two distinct mounting technologies in its catalog: the Mounting Block System, which has shipped on <Mono>Sonnet</Mono> since 2024, and the Crown Mounting System, which is newer and debuted on <Mono>Prologue</Mono> in September 2026. PC Gamer's report — the one driving the W47 tracker move — says Crown is coming to a refreshed <Mono>Encore</Mono> next. It does not say Crown is replacing or related to the Mounting Block System mechanically, and neither does Mode. No pricing, date, or material spec for the Encore refresh has surfaced yet; the report is a roadmap item, not a listing.
</Callout>

The charitable version: this is the first forward-looking Mode news of any kind since the Summer 2026 update, and a vendor row on this tracker scores the whole brand's week, not one SKU in isolation. A refresh on the horizon for Encore — Mode's other board, sitting in the same fulfillment lull as Sonnet — is exactly the kind of signal that pulled the row up to 98 the first time Crown Mounting System made news, back in W40. If Crown's rollout across the catalog is the thing readers are actually waiting on, W47 is a legitimate early tell that the next hype cycle is starting to assemble.

The skeptical version: a three-point move off a 68 floor is a rounding error measured against the 30-point fall from 98 to 68 that preceded it. Nothing about the actual lull — Sonnet and Encore sitting with no restock, no teaser, no new colorway for six straight weeks — is resolved by this report. And the pattern has already failed once: W42's note mentions a 2026 Sonnet refresh "teased in Mode's own compendium," and that tease did nothing to stop the score falling for four more weeks after it appeared. A second-hand press report about a future Encore refresh, with no price, no date, and no confirmed spec, is structurally the same kind of signal — a tease, just one with a PC Gamer byline attached instead of a line in Mode's own newsletter.

<PullQuote>A tease in Mode's own compendium already failed to break this lull once. This one just arrived with better press.</PullQuote>

## What would actually resolve it

The clean test is the next two snapshots. If W48 or W49 brings a concrete Encore listing — a date, a price, a confirmed spec sheet for how Crown Mounting System actually sits inside that chassis — the W47 tick reads as the start of the next real cycle, the same shape as the run-up to Prologue's own 98 in W40. If instead the score drifts back down toward 68 with no further news, W47 was a one-article bump on a vendor row that otherwise has nothing scheduled, and the lull picks back up where it left off. Either way, the next couple of tracker weeks are a cleaner test of which read holds than the W47 number is on its own — [thock's trends tracker](/trends/tracker) is the place to watch it happen, and the Mode catalog background on the Sonnet side of this lull is covered in [thock's prior Sonnet R2 group-buy coverage](/article/mode-sonnet-r2-group-buy-coverage).
