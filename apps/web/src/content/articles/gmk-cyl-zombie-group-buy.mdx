<Mono>GMK CYL Zombie</Mono> is a Halloween-themed keycap set running a group buy jointly through <Mono>KBDfans</Mono> and <Mono>KeyAtlas</Mono>, open October 2 through October 31, 2026. It's also the subject of the single steepest climb on the 2026-W42 trends tracker, and the buy that produced that climb is nowhere near closed — by this piece's October 6, 2026 publish date, it's about a third of the way through its run.

## What's in the set

Zombie follows the genre conventions of a seasonal GMK CYL colorway rather than breaking from them. The base row leans into a near-black charcoal, with a pumpkin-orange alpha set carrying most of the legend contrast. The accent and modifier rows shift into a sickly zombie-green — the kind of acid-tinted hue that horror-themed sets reach for specifically because it reads as "wrong" against a keyboard's usual palette — and the novelties row leans on the genre's standard iconography: stitched grins, tombstones, a bandaged or bitten-looking Esc cap. None of that is unusual for a Halloween run; what's unusual is how fast it's moving on the tracker relative to other seasonal sets thock has covered.

The CYL profile itself — GMK's own Cherry-height mold line, sculpted and spherical-topped rather than cylindrical despite the name — is the same profile family behind most of GMK's recent run of colorway-driven buys, including the football-crest <Mono>Red Devils</Mono> [re-run thock covered last month](/article/gmk-cyl-red-devils-r2-comeback). Zombie doesn't share anything with that set beyond the mold and the GMK branding, but the two runs make a useful point of comparison for how differently a themed colorway can move once it's live.

## A joint listing, open for the full month of October

KBDfans and KeyAtlas are running Zombie as a joint listing rather than either vendor carrying it alone — split fulfillment across two storefronts for the same group buy, open on both pages for the same October 2 to October 31, 2026 window. That's not a novel structure; Red Devils ran the same way across Omnitype and Keebz N Cables, and two-vendor joint listings have become a routine way for GMK-tooled sets expecting above-average order volume to spread fulfillment rather than route everything through a single page. Nothing published alongside the Zombie listing specifies why KBDfans and KeyAtlas chose to split it, so that's as far as the comparison goes — the structure is familiar, the reasoning behind this particular split isn't disclosed.

## The climb, read against the calendar

What put Zombie on thock's radar isn't the theme — it's the shape of its tracker line. The 2026-W42 snapshot has it at a score of 70, direction up, off an eight-week spark trail of 0, 5, 10, 15, 25, 40, 55, 70. Every single read is higher than the one before it; there's no plateau anywhere in the sequence, and the steepest single jump — 15 to 25, then 25 to 40 — lands in the middle of the run rather than tapering off early. The tracker's own note on the row is restrained about why: "Halloween-themed group buy (Oct 2-31) is mid-run across KBDfans and KeyAtlas listings, with seasonal interest typically building as the close date approaches."

That note is a plain, unembellished read: seasonal sets tend to gain interest as their holiday approaches, and Zombie's climb is consistent with that pattern rather than an outlier requiring a separate explanation. It's worth being precise about what the tracker is and isn't saying here. It isn't forecasting a final score, and it isn't claiming the climb will hold its current slope all the way to October 31 — the note describes what's typical for the category, not what's guaranteed for this specific run. By the October 6, 2026 publish date of this piece, the buy had been open for four days and had another twenty-five days to run before its October 31 close — a climb read taken early in the window, not at either end of it.

## Seasonal colorways are a recurring, minor tracker category

Holiday and seasonal-themed keycap sets show up on the tracker often enough to be a recognizable minor pattern rather than a one-off. They tend to follow a similar rhythm: modest attention when the group buy opens, then a climb that tracks the calendar toward whatever date gives the theme its relevance. Zombie's climb fits that shape cleanly — nothing about its trajectory so far breaks from what a seasonal set building toward Halloween would be expected to do. The set is also, on its own terms, a fairly conventional execution of the genre: a dark base, one saturated accent color carrying the theme, a novelties row doing the iconographic heavy lifting. None of that is a knock on the set — it's a description of why a Halloween colorway following a Halloween calendar isn't, on its own, a surprising result.

## What to watch

The open question isn't whether Zombie's score keeps climbing — a seasonal set gaining attention as its holiday nears is the tracker's own baseline expectation, not a surprise worth flagging on its own. The more interesting read is whether the climb holds its current unbroken shape through the final two and a half weeks of the window, or whether it does what the tracker's note implies seasonal sets typically do and steepens further as October 31 gets closer. A continued smooth climb would suggest Zombie is simply riding the calendar on schedule; a late acceleration in the final week would be the more distinctive outcome, and the more useful one for understanding how sharply a seasonal theme can concentrate demand right up against its own deadline. Either way, the [2026-W42 trends tracker](/trends/tracker/2026-W42) is the place to watch it update, and the full category view at [thock's trends page](/trends) is worth checking against whichever other seasonal or colorway-driven sets are moving alongside it.
