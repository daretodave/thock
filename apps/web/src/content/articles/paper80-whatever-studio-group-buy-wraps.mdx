Wuque Studio's own shop listing for the <Mono>Paper80 x Whatever Studio</Mono> still reads "closed." It has read that way since April 14, 2026. thock never ran a companion piece when the buy opened, when it closed, or at any point in the seven months since — a gap in coverage, not a judgment on the board. This is that catch-up, arriving late and in roughly the same low-key register the group buy itself wrapped in.

## What actually happened

The <Mono>Paper80 x Whatever Studio</Mono> is an 80-percent aluminum board built around a lock-hole gasket mount and a mid-case fold inspired by a folded paper airplane, supporting both ANSI and ISO layouts with a choice of PC or aluminum plates. Wuque Studio ran the order window globally from March 31 through April 14, 2026 — a two-week buy, direct through <Source href="https://shop.wuquestudio.com/products/gb-paper-80-whatever-studio">the vendor's own product page</Source>, with no separate regional proxy listing in thock's records the way some of the studio's other runs have used. The vendor's own status field for the record reads "closed," and its description is specific about what that means going forward: the group buy "has wrapped, with limited follow-on stock."

That's the entire confirmed record. Wuque Studio has not published a unit count for the run, a price for whatever follow-on stock remains, or a date for when that stock becomes available. thock looked for all three and came up empty — the vendor's product page is the only primary source attached to this buy, and it doesn't go further than the one line quoted above. Readers chasing a specific number should treat that page, not this article, as the live source once (or if) it updates.

## Why flag a buy that already closed

Most of thock's group-buy coverage runs forward-looking — an order window is live, and there's a clock worth watching. This piece runs the opposite direction on purpose. The <Mono>Paper80 x Whatever Studio</Mono> buy is a genuine gap in thock's own coverage: a closed, global, aluminum-board group buy from a vendor thock covers regularly, with no article ever filed against it. Writing about it now, honestly dated seven months after the fact, is more useful to a reader than pretending it's still news. A reader who searches "Paper80 Whatever Studio" today wants to know what happened and what's left, not a breathless relaunch angle grafted onto an order book that locked in April.

## What "wrapped, with limited follow-on stock" means for a buyer

<Callout type="note" title="Two different transactions, one board">
  A closed group buy and a follow-on-stock sale look similar from a product page but behave differently for a buyer. The group buy was an ordering window against a production run the vendor hadn't built yet — pay in, wait, receive a board built to the locked spec. Follow-on stock, when and if it appears, is Wuque Studio selling whatever the <Mono>Paper80 x Whatever Studio</Mono> production run produced beyond what backers ordered — units that already exist, sold outside the ordering window, in whatever quantity is left over.
</Callout>

The practical read for a buyer is simple: there's no live order window to join, and there's no confirmed restock date to wait for. "Limited follow-on stock" is Wuque Studio's own phrasing for units that may surface for sale outside the original buy, on a timeline and in a quantity the vendor hasn't published. Checking the vendor's own listing page periodically is the only reliable way to catch it.

The one line on that sheet that isn't boilerplate is the fold — a mid-case crease across the shell that reads as a literal paper-airplane fold rather than a decorative chamfer, which is the design hook the "Whatever Studio" partner brought to the collaboration. Wuque Studio hasn't published switch, keycap, or firmware specifics tied to this particular run in the material thock could locate, so this piece stops at the case-and-mount level rather than guessing at the rest of the kit.

## Where this sits in Wuque Studio's pattern

Wuque Studio runs two kinds of boards side by side: an in-house catalog (the kind of grail-chassis lines thock has covered separately in [Wuque Studio's summer update](/article/wuque-studio-summer-update)) and partnered group buys like this one, built with outside designers and sold through the same storefront. The <Mono>Paper80 x Whatever Studio</Mono> sits firmly in the second bucket — a one-off collaboration, run once, closed once, with no indication from the vendor that a second round is coming. ## What to watch

The open question is whether that "limited follow-on stock" ever actually surfaces for sale, and if it does, how it behaves. A quiet restock that clears in minutes would say the April buy undershot real demand for the fold design — a signal Wuque Studio and "Whatever Studio" would likely read as an argument for a second collaboration. A follow-on batch that lingers, or never materializes at all, says the opposite: that the group buy already captured the market for this particular board. Either way, the vendor's own product page is the only place that answer will show up first — thock will treat a confirmed restock date or price as worth a short, separately dated update rather than folding it quietly back into this piece.
