GMK CYL Orange Alert opened for group buy on August 14, 2026, hosted globally by Divinikey with KBDfans, UniKeys, and Keebz N Cables running alongside as additional storefronts. The window runs through September 14, 2026. It is the second set in GMK's CYL "Alert" naming convention to reach a group buy this year, following Prussian Alert's close at KBDfans in June.

## What's in the run

Orange Alert is a <Mono>GMK CYL</Mono> doubleshot ABS set in [Cherry profile](/article/keycap-profiles-compared) — the cylindrical sub-line GMK has run alongside its standard spherical catalog for the past two years. Per the Divinikey listing, the palette pairs a GMK Orenji-matched orange with beige and gray, and layers in Katakana accent legends across the novelties. The result reads industrial and technical rather than decorative — closer to equipment signage than to a themed art set, which is the register GMK has been reaching for with the Alert name specifically.

That register is consistent with the sub-series' first entry. <Mono>GMK CYL Prussian Alert</Mono>, which ran at KBDfans from May 15 through June 12, 2026, paired a deep Prussian red against unbleached cream with an ochre modifier accent — restrained, Bauhaus-leaning, and light on accent colors. Orange Alert follows the same discipline with a different palette: three colors doing the work (orange, beige, gray) rather than a modifier-kit sprawl of secondary tones. Two Alert-series sets landing within a few months of each other, both with the same tight three-to-four-color structure, is enough of a pattern to call it a naming convention rather than a one-off.

Exact kit contents — base, modifier, novelties, spacebar, and whether a deskmat is included — are not itemized in the material available at open. The Divinikey listing is the authoritative source for the full kit breakdown and pricing, and should be checked directly before ordering rather than assumed from precedent.

## Timing

Divinikey is the primary and global-facing vendor for Orange Alert, with KBDfans, UniKeys, and Keebz N Cables listed as additional storefronts — a wider regional spread than a single-host CYL run, though the full vendor map with regional coverage detail sits on the Divinikey product page rather than in the group-buy summary. The order window is thirty-one days, August 14 through September 14, 2026, in line with the four-to-five-week windows recent CYL sets have run.

Estimated shipping is February 2027, per the Divinikey listing — about six months out from the August 14 open, or five months from the September 14 close. That close-to-delivery span runs shorter than the 9-to-12-month range recent <Mono>GMK CYL</Mono> runs like Prussian Alert and Selene have logged; whether Divinikey's estimate holds through the run or slips closer to that established range is one of the things worth watching as the close approaches.

<Callout type="note" title="Multi-vendor structure">
  Orange Alert's four-vendor listing (Divinikey, KBDfans, UniKeys, Keebz N Cables) is broader than a typical single-host CYL buy but narrower than the ten-plus-vendor regional rosters some recent GMK CYL sets have carried. Buyers outside those four storefronts' regions should confirm whether a closer regional proxy exists before ordering direct, since customs and VAT handling falls to the buyer on any cross-border order.
</Callout>

## Why this is on the radar

GMK CYL Orange Alert was tracking at +72 on the [2026-W35 Trends Tracker](/trends/tracker/2026-W35) (week of August 24), direction up — a score in the same range recent breakout switches have carried, not typically what an unreleased keycap set posts ten days into its window. Some of that momentum is inherited from the Alert name itself. <PartReference id="gmk-cyl-selene" /> demonstrated that a tightly-scoped special-effects or palette story can outperform a kit-everything approach on the CYL line, and Prussian Alert's GeekHack interest-check cleared over 120,000 views before its own open. Orange Alert doesn't have a public IC-traffic figure attached in the materials available at open, but it is launching into a market that already has one strong Alert-series precedent to compare against — and the tracker's early climb suggests the comparison is landing.

## What to watch

Buyers weighing Orange Alert against Prussian Alert are choosing between two color stories inside the same sub-line and the same manufacturing pipeline — the decision is closer to picking a palette than picking between competing profiles or plastics. For buyers newer to CYL specifically, the [Cherry-profile explainer](/article/keycap-profiles-compared) covers how the cylindrical sculpt in this profile compares to standard GMK spherical-top sets.

The close on September 14, 2026 will settle the kit list, final pricing, and confirm whether the four-vendor structure holds or expands.
