## Guides — the fork wired firmware doesn't prepare you for

**[ZMK for wireless and split builds: a practical guide](/article/zmk-wireless-split-firmware-guide)**

## Ideas — the mod priced per switch, not per board

**[The Holee mod: what foam inside the switch actually buys you](/article/holee-mod-explained)**

Small polyurethane pads seated inside the switch housing damp an internal resonance no PE foam or tape mod can reach, at a cost of three to five minutes per switch — three to six hours for a 65% board. The piece is upfront that it only pencils out when the switches are already open on the bench for a lube pass.

Read it if you've heard the term thrown around forum threads and want to know exactly which foam layer it means before opening a single switch.
