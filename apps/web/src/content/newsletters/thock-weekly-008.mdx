## Guides — the Enter key shape that decides your keycap set

**[ANSI, ISO, or JIS: a practical layout buying guide](/article/ansi-iso-jis-keyboard-layout-guide)**

Two boards can look nearly identical in a listing photo and still take entirely different keycap sets, and the giveaway is rarely the layout name in the title. The piece walks through reading Enter key shape, bottom-row widths, and spec-sheet shorthand before ordering, not after a set arrives that doesn't fit the board it was bought for.

Read it if you've ever bought a keycap set for a board you didn't spec yourself.

## Ideas — what a socket actually costs

**[Hot-swap sockets: what you actually give up for the convenience](/article/hot-swap-sockets-tradeoffs)**

Tool-free switch swapping turned hot-swap PCBs into the default spec across the hobby, and the piece treats that as earned before it gets specific about the price: contact reliability against a soldered joint, socket wear after repeated swaps, a feel that's measurably softer under the stem. It also names the two builds where soldering is still the right call.

Read it if hot-swap has become your default and you want the tradeoff spelled out instead of assumed.
